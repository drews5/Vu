Vocal U A Cappella

We Are Vocal U

Vocal U is a gender-inclusive a cappella group at the University of Minnesota, established in 2011. We are a registered student organization dedicated to spreading our music across the Twin Cities and beyond, and having a great time while doing it.

We come from all different majors and backgrounds, but we're all a part of VU because we love music and the arts. More than an a cappella group, Vocal U is a family. We support and push each other to be the best performers we can be, which translates to the stage.

About Us

What do you sing?

Our repertoire includes Back to Black, Ribs, Wings, and a Phineas & Ferb medley. Each semester, we carry over half of our existing songs and choose new ones by a vote.

Our Repertoire

Where can I see you perform?

We perform at U of M events, charity events, and around the Twin Cities. Our events page has upcoming performances and past shows. You can also watch our performances on YouTube.

Events

Can I book Vocal U for an event?

Yes! We love collaborating with other groups and performing for the community. Email vocalu@umn.edu with the date, location, and a little about your event to ask about booking us.

Book Us for a Gig

How do I audition?

Check our audition page for dates and updates. We also post audition announcements on Instagram at @vocal_u. If you have a question about auditions, email us at vocalu@umn.edu.
