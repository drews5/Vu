Vocal U A Cappella

Vocal U is a gender-inclusive a cappella group at the University of Minnesota. We perform at campus events, charity events, showcases, competitions, and throughout the Twin Cities.

Founded in 2011, our group shares a love of music and community through student-led a cappella performance.

Explore our mission and repertoire, current members, events, and performances.

Good to know

What is Vocal U?

Vocal U is a gender-inclusive, student-led a cappella group at the University of Minnesota. Founded in 2011, we bring students from different majors and backgrounds together through vocal music.

Read about our mission

Where does Vocal U perform?

We perform at University of Minnesota events, charity events, showcases, competitions, and community events in Minneapolis and the wider Twin Cities area.

Can I book Vocal U for an event?

Yes. Send us your event date, location, and the kind of performance you have in mind. We can discuss availability and details with you.

How can I audition for Vocal U?

Audition information changes by semester. Check our audition page for the current update and follow our social channels for announcements.

Who are the current members?

Our current roster includes sopranos, mezzos, altos, tenors, baritone/basses, and vocal percussion. The members page lists everyone by part.
