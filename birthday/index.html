Birthday songs with Vocal U

Give someone a birthday song from Vocal U. We can send a personalized video or come sing for them in person.

We're a self-funded student group, and every birthday song helps us make music and put on more shows at the U.

Birthday video — $5

We'll sing Happy Birthday with their name and send you the video to share.

Let us know their name and when you need the video.

In-person song — $50

We'll come to their dorm, apartment, or wherever you're celebrating to sing Happy Birthday or Birthday Sex.

Let us know where and about what time.

Request a birthday song

Use the request form to send your name, email, their name, the date, and any other details. We'll reply by email to confirm the details and send our Venmo.
