# Vocal U web design

The site should feel like a real student music group: joyful, a little unexpected, polished enough for bookings and donors, and recognizably collegiate. Vocal U is not a corporate brand. The site should feel made by people who like singing together.

## Identity

- Vocal blue: `#91A8C6`
- Navy: `#2E4C6D`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Yearbook Solid for page and section headings
- Inter for body copy, navigation, forms, and controls

Use title or sentence case. Yearbook Solid should stay at its regular weight.

## Interface

- Let candid group photography carry the visual personality. Crops can overlap, tilt, and break a grid when it helps a page feel alive.
- The floating navigation island, attached audition tab, layered imagery, oversized Yearbook type, and small musical details are signature elements. Preserve them.
- Use blue gradients, playful stickers, rounded shapes, and occasional off-axis elements with restraint—not uniform card grids on every page.
- Mix corner radii and depth intentionally. A slightly oversized radius or offset shadow can make a featured moment feel tactile.
- Motion may communicate state or add personality. Keep it brief, springy, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Add small moments of delight where they fit, especially around auditions, events, and member photography.
- Avoid generic startup patterns, vague slogans, identical section templates, and anything that makes the group feel like a corporate service.
- Write specific, conversational copy and avoid generic marketing language.

## Accessibility

- Maintain visible keyboard focus.
- Keep body text at 16 pixels when space allows.
- Give icon-only controls an accessible label.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not place buttons or links inside other interactive elements.
