# Vocal U web design

The site should feel like a real student music group: polished enough for bookings and donors, but warm, direct, and recognizably collegiate.

## Identity

- Vocal blue: `#91A8C6`
- Navy: `#2E4C6D`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Yearbook Solid for page and section headings
- Inter for body copy, navigation, forms, and controls

Use title or sentence case. Yearbook Solid should stay at its regular weight.

## Interface

- Let group photography carry the visual personality.
- Prefer solid color, spacing, and typography over decorative gradients.
- Use rounded corners consistently, usually 12 to 16 pixels.
- Keep shadows light and limited to overlays or hover feedback.
- Use motion only when it clarifies a state change.
- Write specific, conversational copy and avoid generic marketing language.

## Accessibility

- Maintain visible keyboard focus.
- Keep body text at 16 pixels when space allows.
- Give icon-only controls an accessible label.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not place buttons or links inside other interactive elements.
